Completed files were automatically uploaded to SharePoint with email notifications containing direct links. Both halves are hard on their own: RPA is one discipline and advanced Excel VBA is another. Combining them is where most RPA developers stop, because they do not know what VBA can do or how to write it. Here the bot and the workbook were designed as one system from the start, which is why the result replicated cleanly."
